Topics I am interested in or research on

Pseudo-differential and Fourier Integral Operators

My work in this area concerns algebraic properties of the calculi of pseudo-differential operators on noncompact and singular manifolds. I have extensively studied the case of asymptotically Euclidean structures and the SG and scattering calculus. In this context I study the relation with Fourier Integral Operators and symplectic gometry on singular manifolds. This is the subject of my PhD thesis and of this article.

Spectral theory on manifolds

I am interested in the study of asymptotics of the L^2-spectrum of elliptic operators on manifolds with boundary and other singularities. I have written a review about the Dirichlet Laplacian, available at arXiv. I am currently working on a relativistic version of this formula with Alexander Strohmaier.

Algebraic quantum field theory on curved spacetimes

BOundary-value problems for normally and Green hyperbolic operators on spacetimes with boundary are a recent line of research, especially in connection with microlocal analysis and algebraic quantum field theory. In this context, I have a joint preprint with Alexander Strohmaier, in which we construct a class of pyhsical quantum states on the Klein-Gordon field algebra with Dirichlet boundary condition on a globally hyperbolic spacetime with timelike boundary. There, we use techniques from spectral theory and microlocal analysis to show that any such data gives rise to a pure, quasi-free state with positive energy (as encoded by the b-wave-front set).
